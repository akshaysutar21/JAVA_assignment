# voice.md — One Minute English

YouTube Shorts + Instagram Reels for Hindi speakers learning English. Built from the channel's house
style; no videos yet, so revise this once the first ten are up and you know what you actually sound
like.

## Who I am talking to

One person: a Hindi-speaking twenty-something — a college student, a fresher heading into
interviews, or someone in their first office job — who understands a lot of English but freezes when
they have to speak it. They translate from Hindi in their head and are scared of sounding wrong in
front of a boss, an interviewer or a foreigner. They scroll in Hinglish. They will give you 60
seconds, not 10 minutes, and they leave the moment it feels like a classroom.

## How I actually talk

Hinglish, like a friend explaining something over chai — warm, quick, a bit funny, never talking
down. Short punchy lines, varied length. Hindi for the talking, English only for the thing being
taught.

Sample lines in the house style:

- "अच्छा बताओ... दिमाग का दही — इंग्लिश में क्या बोलोगे?"
- "ये मत बोल देना यार! बोलो — *You're driving me crazy!*"
- "सच-सच बताना, ये गलती आपने भी की है ना?"
- "सामने वाला सच में कटोरी लेके आ जाएगा।"
- "पाँचवीं वाली तो आपके बॉस भी बोलते हैं।"

For list reels, one line per item: "<Hindi description> को <English word> कहते हैं।"

## Words I never use

- Announcer / listicle phrasing: "नंबर दो —", "आज का वर्ड है", "In this video we will learn",
  "Hello friends, welcome back"
- Fillers and warm-ups of any kind — no channel intro, no "before we start"
- YouTube clichés: "game-changer", "unlock", "dive in", "let's get into it", "mind-blowing"
- Anything that mocks the viewer's English ("गंवार", "शर्म आनी चाहिए")

## Words I do use

"अच्छा बताओ…", "यार", "ना?", "सच-सच बताना", "ऐसे नहीं, ऐसे बोलो", "सोचो…", "save करो",
"scroll करते-करते English सीखो", "Follow One Minute English". Reactions and small jokes are part of
the voice — the funny literal translation before the real one is the signature move.

## What I will not claim

- Fluency in X days, guaranteed jobs, guaranteed interview selection, salary numbers
- Any English usage, meaning or idiom equivalence that hasn't been fact-checked
- That an Indian-English phrase is "wrong" when it's just regional — say natives may misunderstand
  it, not that the viewer is stupid
- Pronunciation or accent lessons (the voice can't demonstrate them reliably)
- Results, follower counts or testimonials that are not real

## My format

- **Length:** 45–70 seconds, vertical 1080×1920. Shorts and Reels only for now.
- **On camera:** faceless. Animated text cards on a light orange-and-white theme (always light, never
  dark), clean female voiceover, light beat, pop / buzz / ding / tick sound effects.
- **On-screen text:** Hindi in Devanagari; English only for the word or sentence being taught. Short.
- **Opening:** hook text visible in the first second plus a curiosity line, and an open loop
  ("पाँचवीं वाली तो…"). No intro.
- **Formats, rotated:** challenge/quiz with a 2-second timer (Hindi → funny literal ❌ → real
  English ✅); common Indian-English mistakes ❌/✅; word of the day with a relatable story;
  situations ("interview में ऐसे नहीं, ऐसे बोलो"); smart replies to "How are you?" / "Thank you" /
  "Sorry".
- **Close, one ask:** an easy comment (a score, a number, their own muhavra for the next part) +
  "PDF चाहिए? Comment करो PDF, DM में आएगी" + save + Follow One Minute English.
- **Swearing:** none.
